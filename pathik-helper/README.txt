Pathik helper for Innvela
================================

Fills Pathik's "Add Guest" form (pathik.guru) with a guest from Innvela:
name, address, date of birth, mobile, email, journey, room, dates and times,
adults and children, vehicle, ID type and number, the ID copies (made smaller
than 250 KB), and the details of every other adult in the room.

Aadhaar is always added by hand in Pathik: nothing from an Aadhaar is saved
in Innvela. Nothing is submitted for you: check the form, then press
Submit in Pathik.

Install (once, in Google Chrome on the front-desk computer)
1. Unzip this file (right-click > Extract all). Keep the pathik-helper folder.
2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
3. Press "Load unpacked" and choose the pathik-helper folder.

Use
1. In Innvela open the booking (or the guest) and press "Fill in Pathik".
2. Press "Open Pathik and fill the form". Pathik opens in a new tab.
   If Pathik asks you to sign in, sign in and open Add Guest: the form is
   filled as soon as it opens.
3. Filled boxes have a green border; boxes still empty have an amber border.
   A small panel at the bottom right lists what is left to do.
4. Press Submit in Pathik. Then press "Entered in Pathik" in Innvela.

If dates or times come out in the wrong format, choose another format in the
panel and press "Fill again".
